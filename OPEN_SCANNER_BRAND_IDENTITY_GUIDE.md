## 1. Brand Philosophy & Core Identity

Open Scanner was founded on the fundamental principle that global search and information retrieval should be universally accessible, mathematically transparent, and liberated from biased gatekeeping.

### 1.1 Five Brand Pillars
1. **Open:** Open architecture, verifiable crawl indexing, and non-discriminatory access.
2. **Discover:** Intuitive exploration crossing web, news, media, and multimodal intelligence.
3. **Connect:** Seamless identity linking users with services, apps, and creators worldwide.
4. **Create:** Empowering developers, publishers, and creators with unmetered access to discovery.
5. **Trust:** Absolute privacy by default, zero tracking of unauthenticated queries, and cryptographic security.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

The Open Scanner typographic system uses dynamic scale ratios engineered for maximum legibility across ultra-dense search results and expansive hero layouts.

- **Hero / Display (Home Brand):** 44px – 56px, SemiBold / Bold (Tracking: -0.03em)
- **H1 / Modal Titles:** 22px – 26px, Bold (Line-height: 1.25)
- **H2 / Section Headers:** 17px – 19px, SemiBold (Line-height: 1.3)
- **Search Query Input:** 16px (prevents automatic iOS zoom), Regular
- **SERP Result Title:** 18px – 20px, Medium / SemiBold (Color: `#1a0dab` / `#2563eb`)
- **Snippet / Body Text:** 14px, Regular (Line-height: 1.55, Color: `#334155`)
- **Metadata & Badges:** 11px – 12px, Medium (Letter-spacing: +0.02em)

## 4. UI Component Architecture & 4px Spacing Grid

All layout dimensions, paddings, and margins adhere strictly to an 8-point base grid with 4-point micro-adjustments:
- `4px` (`var(--spacing-xxs)`): Badge padding, micro icon margins.
- `8px` (`var(--spacing-xs)`): Button internal padding, tab gaps.
- `12px` (`var(--spacing-sm)`): Form group gaps, list item spacing.
- `16px` (`var(--spacing-md)`): Card padding, input height offsets.
- `24px` (`var(--spacing-lg)`): Modal internal margins, SERP hit spacing.
- `32px` (`var(--spacing-xl)`): Section separators, desktop hero offsets.
